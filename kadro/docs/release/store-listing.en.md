### Description

```text
Organizing a pitch match should not turn into a group-chat maze. Kadro keeps your team, your matches and your search for missing players in one app. Kadro is built for Turkish amateur football ("halı saha"): venues and community content are in Turkey, and the app interface is available in Turkish and English.

BUILD YOUR TEAM
- Create a team and assign a captain and co-captains.
- Add players with an invite link or QR code.

ORGANIZE THE MATCH
- Set the day, time, venue, format (such as 7v7) and number of slots.
- See who is coming and who is not; a waiting list takes over when the match is full.
- Prepare the lineup.
- Split the total pitch fee by the number of players who show up and mark who has paid.
- Vote for the MVP after the match.

EKSIK VAR (MISSING PLAYER CALLS)
- When you are one player short, post a call, review applications and accept the right player.
- Apply to other teams' calls and join their match.

VENUE GUIDE
- Browse venues by district, see features and price range, and read venue reviews.
- Call a venue, then organize a match at that venue.
- Suggest a venue that is not in the guide yet.

IN YOUR CONTROL
- If you allow notifications you get match reminders; permission is optional.
- Sign in with email and password, Sign in with Apple or Google.
- Delete your account from within the app. If you have a Kadro Pro subscription, cancel it separately in the App Store or Google Play.
- Turkish and English interface.

Kadro is a portfolio project; this listing is sample store copy.
```
