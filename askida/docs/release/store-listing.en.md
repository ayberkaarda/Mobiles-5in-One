## Full description

Askıda brings the Turkish "askıda ekmek" (bread on the hook) tradition to your phone. Pay ahead
for something at a bakery, restaurant, stationer or greengrocer, the shop hangs it on its hook,
and someone who needs it walks in and takes it, with no questions asked.

One app, three ways to use it.

LEAVE ONE (donors)
- See verified shops near you, pick an item and a quantity.
- Pay on the payment provider's secure page. Card details never reach the app or our server.
- Once the payment is confirmed the items hang on the shop's hook and a receipt is e-mailed to you.
- Who takes an item is never recorded; you only get an "item taken" notice.
- The money goes to the shop through the payment provider; the platform only books its commission. The commission rate is a sample and is set before launch.

TAKE ONE
- No account, no name, no e-mail. A random identifier tied to this device is used.
- See nearby shops and what is on the hook; your location is used approximately, only to show nearby shops.
- Pick an item and get a one-time code. It is valid for 10 minutes: show it at the shop and take the item.
- Up to two items a day, one per shop.
- "Verilerimi sıfırla" deletes this device's anonymous identity at any time.

SHOPKEEPERS
- Register your shop, upload your documents, and open items on the hook once verified.
- Scan or type the code; hand over the item and the record closes in one step.
- Follow payouts and history in the app.

Why it is safe
- Shops are verified with documents; an unverified shop is never shown.
- Every code works once and returns to the hook when it expires.
- The person who takes shows no identity to the shop or the donor and is never rated.
- You can delete your account from the app.

The app is Turkish first and also available in English. This release is a portfolio build: shops,
addresses and figures are samples and are marked [ÖRNEK].
