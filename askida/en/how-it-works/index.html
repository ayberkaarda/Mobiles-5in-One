How Askıda works

A donor pays for an item in the app and it is added to the shop rail. Anyone who wants one picks a nearby shop without opening an account, receives a one-time code of 8 characters and shows it at the shop within 10 minutes. The shop scans it and hands over the item; who took it is never recorded.

Three steps, one rail

The limits

Code validity
10 minutes, one use.
Daily limit
2 items per device, 1 per shop.
Search radius
3 km by default, 5 km at most, from an approximate location.
No account
Taking an item needs no account, no identity and no rating.
